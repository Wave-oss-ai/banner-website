Sparkman Animal Clinic has been part of the Muldrow community since 1988. As a mixed-animal veterinary practice, the clinic cares for household companions and supports local livestock owners, too.

Because good veterinary care is about more than an appointment. It's about listening, explaining, and helping you make thoughtful choices for the animals who depend on you.

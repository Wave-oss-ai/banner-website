Frequently asked questions

GOOD TO KNOW.

Is an appointment automatically confirmed?

No. Demo requests stay pending until authorized staff approve them. No real customer bookings are accepted.

Does a message go to the repair shop?

No. This demo stores fictional examples temporarily on a separate server for staff to review. Real messaging requires the business owner's permission and reliable secure storage.

Can the business change its services and hours later?

The website maintainer can update hours and services after the owner confirms them. The demo does not include a self-service business settings editor.

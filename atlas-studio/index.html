Good to know

Common
questions.

Do you use the same template for every business?

No. We start with the business, its customers, and the experience those customers need. Our portfolio concepts illustrate distinctly different visual directions.

Can you build an appointment request system?

Yes. Appointment requests, staff review, and confirmation workflows can be included in a custom project. Actual bookings are only enabled after the business reviews and approves the system and its data-handling process.

Will I own my website?

Ownership, source-code handoff, hosting access, and ongoing support are covered in the written project agreement. We'll make those terms clear before you pay.

How much will my website cost?

It depends on the number of pages, integrations, content, and support you need. We quote each project individually before starting paid work.

Can I see a preview before launch?

Yes. Review and feedback are part of the process, and the business approves the final site before it becomes the official website.

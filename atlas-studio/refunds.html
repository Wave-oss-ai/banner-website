Payments & refunds

Atlas Web Solutions does not collect payments or activate recurring billing through this website.

Quotes and invoices

Projects are quoted individually. Before any charge, the customer and provider should agree in writing on what will be delivered, billing milestones, cancellation terms, any hosting or maintenance subscription, and the refund terms.

Refunds and cancellations

Refund eligibility depends on the signed project agreement, the work already completed, and applicable law. No fixed nonrefundable-deposit or cancellation rule is imposed by this portfolio page. Customers should review their written agreement before making a payment.

Questions

Payment contact information will be provided on the actual invoice or project agreement. This policy will be updated before online checkout is activated.
